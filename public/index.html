What a Chatbot Can’t Do

Plenty of AI will walk a student through a problem. Three things separate a tutor that knows your child from one that doesn’t.

What they’ve mastered, what’s still shaky, how they read, what they’re into. Session two starts where session one ended — and the word problems are about skateboarding if that’s what they care about.
