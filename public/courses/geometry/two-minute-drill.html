Two-Minute Drill

Every play is one line of a proof. Pick the reason that justifies it. Fast and right moves the ball down the field.

Quick & rightUnder 6 seconds: +15 yards. Under 12: +10. Slower: +5.
Wrong = sackLose 5 yards. The clock stops so you can read why.
Touchdown = 7Reach the end zone, score 7, and the next drive starts at your own 25.
