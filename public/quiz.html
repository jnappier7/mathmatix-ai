The fix: think GEMS, not PEMDAS

G
GroupsWhatever is inside parentheses, brackets, fraction bars.
E
ExponentsPowers and roots.
M
Multiply & DivideOne team, same rank — left to right.
S
Subtract & AddAlso one team, same rank — left to right.
Why PEMDAS traps people: six letters make it look like six separate steps, so M seems to beat D and A seems to beat S. They don’t — they’re ties, broken left to right. GEMS bakes that in.
